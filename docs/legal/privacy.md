# Privacy Policy

Arbiter is a Discord bot that helps teams design web pages together. This page explains what it stores and why.

## What we store
- **Design threads.** Messages posted in threads Arbiter created (feedback, votes, approvals), the prototypes it generates, screenshots, and the decision log. This is the product: the thread is the record.
- **Server settings.** Your server's plan, remaining renders, and, if you set one, your own API key, encrypted at rest.
- **Brand memory.** Design tokens, shared page components, rules and the site map derived from pages your team approved, kept per server so later pages stay consistent.
- **Voice, only when you invite it.** If someone runs `/voice join`, speech in that voice channel is transcribed and posted into the design thread, attributed to the speaker, until `/voice leave`, "Arbiter, leave", or the channel empties. Audio is not stored; transcripts are.

## What we don't do
- We do not read messages outside threads Arbiter created, except a message that mentions Arbiter to start a session.
- We do not sell or share your data. Generated content is sent to the AI providers we use (Anthropic for text and images, ElevenLabs or OpenAI for speech) to do the work, under their terms.
- We do not train models on your data.

## Deleting data
`/forget` in a design thread deletes that thread's data. Removing the bot from your server stops all collection. Free-plan sessions are deleted after 30 days of inactivity. Email us to delete everything for a server.
